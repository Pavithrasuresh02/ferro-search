Haar-like Features

Rectangles of light and dark pixels, such as the darker eye band above brighter cheeks. A feature's value is the sum under the white area minus the sum under the black area.

Integral Image

Each pixel stores the sum of everything above and to its left, so any rectangle sum needs just four lookups regardless of size.

AdaBoost

Picks the few most useful features out of thousands and combines many weak classifiers into one strong classifier.

Cascade Classifier

Stages run from cheap to complex. Most background windows are rejected in the first stages, which makes detection fast.

DeepFace

DeepFace (Facebook, 2014) verifies faces with a deep neural network trained on millions of faces.

FaceNet

FaceNet (Google, 2015) maps a face to a compact embedding so that distance directly reflects similarity.

FaceNet Educational Demonstration
FACE IMAGE↓CNN↓FACE EMBEDDING↓DISTANCE↓VERIFICATION

No FaceNet model runs here. The metrics below are computed on the same stand-in 256-value pixel descriptor as the DeepFace section, not on real FaceNet embeddings.

Anchor

A reference face image.

Positive

Another image of the same person as the anchor.

An image of a different person.

Triplet loss

L = max(‖f(a) − f(p)‖² − ‖f(a) − f(n)‖² + α, 0). Training pulls the positive close and pushes the negative at least a margin α away.
